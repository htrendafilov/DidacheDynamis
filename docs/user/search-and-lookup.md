# Search, Navigation & Cross-References

The app provides fast passage lookup, high-speed Full-Text Search (FTS5), and Treasury of Scripture Knowledge (TSK) cross-references.

![Search Results & Cross-Reference Popover](assets/search_and_lookup_illustration.jpg)

## Quick Passage Navigation

- **Book & Chapter Selector**: Click the header passage dropdown to jump directly to any book, chapter, or verse range (e.g., *Genesis 1*, *Matthew 5:1-12*).
- **Previous / Next Chapter**: Use the bottom action arrows or keyboard shortcuts (`Alt + LeftArrow`, `Alt + RightArrow`) to flip between chapters.

## Full-Text Search (FTS)

Access the search tool via the magnifying glass icon or pressing `/`:
- **Keyword Search**: Type words or phrases (e.g. `light of the world`).
- **Exact Match**: Use double quotes for exact phrases (e.g. `"in the beginning"`).
- **Search Scopes**: Filter results by Old Testament, New Testament, or specific books.

## Cross-References & Dictionary Lookups

- **TSK Cross-References**: Click on any verse number or cross-reference icon to open related verses with inline text previews.
- **Easton's Dictionary**: Select any highlighted key term or open a Dictionary pane to look up historical and biblical definitions.
