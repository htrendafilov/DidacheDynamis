# M9.0b-1 — Estimator calibration across tokenizer families

Parent: [`../interactive_chat_plan.md`](../interactive_chat_plan.md) §8.3, §15
Closes: [`m9.0-findings.md`](m9.0-findings.md) §5a
Runs: **inside M9.3, week 1. Blocks M9.3 step 2 (`tokens.ts`).**
Effort: half a day

## Why this runs before M9.3 step 2, not after M9.3

M9.3's suggested order builds `tokens.ts` at step 2 of 7 and `context.ts` at step 3. `context.ts` enforces the §8.3 budget — 8,000 tokens of assembled context, 2,000 per source, 12 sources — entirely through the divisors this task measures. If the divisors move after step 3 lands, the budgeting logic and every one of its tests move with them.

Half a day now costs less than reworking step 3 later. That is the whole argument.

## The gap being closed

`m9.0-findings.md` §5 calibrated against **two Gemini-family models** and covered everything else with a **×1.15 multiplier that was never measured**. That is not a theoretical hole: §9 of the same document records five consecutive `openrouter/free` requests answered by five different models, spanning NVIDIA Nemotron and Cohere — the exact families the multiplier is guessing at.

An under-count here silently overruns the context budget, which is the one thing the estimator exists to prevent.

### 1a. No Bulgarian scripture in the corpus — permanently

There is no permissible source for one. [`../content_and_licensing.md`](../content_and_licensing.md) records no cleared open Bulgarian Bible (no public-domain Bulgarian module on CrossWire; BulVeren/BulCarigradNT grants do not extend to us; ББД and BG1940 are not cleared).

And the direction of travel makes this permanent rather than temporary: if a Bulgarian Bible is deployed at all it arrives under a **negotiated, non-open licence** — `allowed_no_training` at best — whose text must never be sent to an external AI service, let alone committed to a public benchmark corpus. A sample that exists only until a licence is signed and then has to be ripped out is worse than no sample.

#10 therefore uses the **CC0 Bulgarian 1689 Baptist Confession** (merged via PR #19): formal theological prose in the target register, public-domain dedication, already cleared for AI use. If it is ever unavailable when this runs, substitute another verified-CC0 Bulgarian theological text and record the substitution in `bench/README.md`; do not reach for a Bible text "just for calibration".

The English samples are public domain or CrossWire-licensed (WEB, Matthew Henry, Easton's, Strong's, TSK), so the corpus as a whole is `ai_context_policy = allowed` and safe to check in.

## 2. Measure

Send each prompt with `max_tokens: 1` and `usage: {include: true}`; read `usage.prompt_tokens`.

Pin **one model per tokenizer family**. Family coverage is the point, not model count:

| Family | Suggested pin | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Gemini | `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite` through OpenRouter | Already measured; the control |
| Llama / Nemotron | any current `nvidia/nemotron-*` entry | Answered `openrouter/free` requests in findings §9 |
| Cohere | `cohere/north-mini-code` | Same |
| Qwen | any current Qwen entry | Third major family in the free pool |

Record `chars / prompt_tokens` per (prompt × family) into `bench/results-<date>.json`, raw.

## 3. Decide

Then either:

- **Confirm** the `1.15` multiplier in `chat/tokens.ts` covers the worst case across all four families — and replace the comment "covers other tokenizer families" with the measurement that proves it; or
- **Raise it** to whatever the worst case requires, and say so in the same comment.

**The estimate must never be below actual on any sample.** That exit criterion does not change.

### If a family cannot be measured

`m9.0-findings.md` §5a option (b) applies: constrain the model picker to measured families and treat the rest as unsupported. This is the cheap escape if the task slips — it is a one-line filter.

It costs `openrouter/free`, since a dynamic router cannot promise a family. That is a smaller sacrifice than it first appears: **no default model ships**, so the user is already choosing a model explicitly.
